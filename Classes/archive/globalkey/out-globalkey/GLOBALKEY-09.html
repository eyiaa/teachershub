At Work Right Now: Present Continuous

3-PILLAR CURRICULUM FRAMEWORK

Class: GLOBALKEY-09   Student name: ____________________   CEFR phase: Global Key (A2)

Target goal: Describe actions happening now using am, is, or are with the -ing form.

Can-do objective: Can describe what people are doing now at work or in a familiar place using simple present continuous sentences.

Balance: Skeleton timing: Warm-up 10% | Theory 25-30% | Comprehension 25-30% | Conversation 25-30% | Wrap-up 5-10%

StageTimeShare
1. Warm-Up & Lead-In3 min10%
2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)8 min27%

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a picture of an office with people working. Ask: What are the people doing right now?

Tutor action: Give a short example: The woman is typing an email. Let the student answer freely.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing (happening now or around now)

Examples:

  • I am writing a report at the moment.
  • He is talking on the phone.
  • They are having a meeting in the room.

Execution method: Show the formula with the office picture. Ask concept-checking questions: Is this happening now? Which form of to be follows he? Then ask the student to describe one person in the picture.

Check question: Do we say he is work or he is working?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice at a steady pace: It is ten o'clock at the hotel. The manager is checking the reservations. Two receptionists are answering the phones, and a cleaner is preparing the rooms.

Direct fact check

Ask: What is the manager doing? (checking the reservations). What are the receptionists doing? (answering the phones).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does the text describe a calm or busy place? Accept a simple answer and ask the student to point to the phrase that shows it, such as are answering the phones.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Picture description: the student describes three people in the office picture to the tutor, using the present continuous. The tutor asks: What is the person next to them doing?

Target phrases / vocab

  • He is ...
  • They are ...
  • She is ...

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Use the office picture on screen and praise each complete description before correcting.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • She is work on the computer.
  • They are write an email.

Good vs. native-like upgrades

  • Accurate: She is work on the computer.
    Native: She is working on the computer.
  • Accurate: They are write an email.
    Native: They are writing an email.

Checklist

  • ☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?
  • ☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?
  • ☐ Did the lesson touch all three pillars?
  • ☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
