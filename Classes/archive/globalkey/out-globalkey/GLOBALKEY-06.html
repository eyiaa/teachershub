Yesterday Was Busy: Past Simple of To Be and Regular Verbs

3-PILLAR CURRICULUM FRAMEWORK

Class: GLOBALKEY-06   Student name: ____________________   CEFR phase: Global Key (A2)

Target goal: Describe yesterday using was, were, and regular past verbs.

Can-do objective: Can describe past events and experiences using simple sentences about familiar topics.

Balance: Skeleton timing: Warm-up 10% | Theory 25-30% | Comprehension 25-30% | Conversation 25-30% | Wrap-up 5-10%

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a calendar with yesterday highlighted. Ask: What did you do yesterday?

Tutor action: Give a short example: I worked in the morning. Let the student answer freely.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: I / he / she / it + was; we / you / they + were; verb + ed for regular verbs (worked, played)

Examples:

  • I was at home yesterday.
  • They were late for the meeting.
  • We worked in the garden and cooked lunch.

Execution method: Show the was and were forms with the regular past verb formula. Ask concept-checking questions: Which form follows they? Is worked a present or past form? Then ask the student to say one thing they did yesterday.

Check question: Do we say they was late or they were late?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice at a steady pace: Yesterday was a busy day for Ana. She was at work until five. Then she walked home and cooked dinner with her brother.

Direct fact check

Ask: When was Ana at work? (until five). What did she do after work? (walked home and cooked dinner).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Was Ana's day relaxed or busy? Accept a simple answer and ask the student to point to a word that shows it, such as busy.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Yesterday story: the student tells the tutor about yesterday in three sentences, using was or were in one and two regular past verbs. The tutor asks: What did you do after that?

Target phrases / vocab

  • Yesterday I was ...
  • Then I worked ...
  • After that, I ...

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Give the student time to finish each sentence and praise each complete past sentence.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • Yesterday I am at home.
  • I walk to school yesterday.

Good vs. native-like upgrades

  • Accurate: Yesterday I am at home.
    Native: Yesterday I was at home.
  • Accurate: I walk to school yesterday.
    Native: I walked to school yesterday.

Checklist

  • ☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?
  • ☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?
  • ☐ Did the lesson touch all three pillars?
  • ☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
