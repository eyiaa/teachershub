Skills and Abilities: Can and Can't

3-PILLAR CURRICULUM FRAMEWORK

Class: GLOBALKEY-04   Student name: ____________________   CEFR phase: Global Key (A1)

Target goal: Say what you can and cannot do using can and can't with a base verb.

Can-do objective: Can talk about personal abilities and skills in simple sentences and understand short descriptions of them.

Balance: Skeleton timing: Warm-up 10% | Theory 25-30% | Comprehension 25-30% | Conversation 25-30% | Wrap-up 5-10%

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a picture of someone swimming and someone cooking. Ask: Can you swim? Can you cook?

Tutor action: Model a short answer: I can swim, but I can't cook well. Let the student answer freely.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Subject + can / can't + base verb (no to, no s)

Examples:

  • I can speak two languages.
  • She can drive, but she can't ride a bike.
  • We can't come to the party on Friday.

Execution method: Show the formula with the two example types. Ask concept-checking questions: Which verb form follows can? Do we add s after she? Then ask the student to say one thing they can do and one they can't do.

Check question: Do we say she can drives or she can drive?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice at a steady pace: Tom is a very good cook. He can make pasta and soup. He can't swim, but he can run very fast.

Direct fact check

Ask: What can Tom make? (pasta and soup). What can't Tom do? (swim). What can he do very fast? (run).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does Tom seem proud of his skills? Accept a simple answer and ask the student to point to the word that shows the feeling, such as very good.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Skills exchange: the student tells the tutor three things they can do and one thing they cannot do. The tutor asks one follow-up question: Can you show me?

Target phrases / vocab

  • I can ...
  • I can't ...
  • Can you ...?

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Use the screen pictures to prompt the student, and praise each complete sentence.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • She can drives a car.
  • I cans speak English.

Good vs. native-like upgrades

  • Accurate: She can drives a car.
    Native: She can drive a car.
  • Accurate: I cans speak English.
    Native: I can speak English.

Checklist

  • ☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?
  • ☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?
  • ☐ Did the lesson touch all three pillars?
  • ☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
