Around Town: There Is and There Are

3-PILLAR CURRICULUM FRAMEWORK

Class: GLOBALKEY-03   Student name: ____________________   CEFR phase: Global Key (A1)

Target goal: Describe what is in a place and where things are using there is, there are, and next to.

Can-do objective: Can describe places in a town and give simple directions using basic prepositions.

Balance: Skeleton timing: Warm-up 10% | Theory 25-30% | Comprehension 25-30% | Conversation 25-30% | Wrap-up 5-10%

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a street picture with a bank, a park, and a café. Ask: What is in your neighbourhood?

Tutor action: Point to the picture and give a short example: There is a park near my house. Let the student answer freely.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: There is + singular noun / There are + plural noun + place (in, on, next to)

Examples:

  • There is a bank on the main street.
  • There are two cafés next to the park.
  • There is a bus stop near the school.

Execution method: Show the street picture with labels. Ask concept-checking questions: Is the noun singular or plural? Which form do we use with two cafés? Then ask the student to describe one place in the picture.

Check question: Do we say there is two cafés or there are two cafés?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this description twice at a steady pace: In our town, there is a big library. There are three shops on the high street. The post office is next to the bank.

Direct fact check

Ask: How many shops are on the high street? (three). What is next to the bank? (the post office).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does the speaker like the town? Accept a simple answer and ask the student to point to the word that shows the opinion, such as big.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Town guide: the student describes three places in the picture to the tutor using there is or there are, then gives directions from the bank to the café using next to.

Target phrases / vocab

  • There is a ...
  • There are ...
  • It is next to ...

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Use the street picture on screen and praise each complete description before correcting.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • There is two shops.
  • The café is next the park.

Good vs. native-like upgrades

  • Accurate: There is two shops.
    Native: There are two shops.
  • Accurate: The café is next the park.
    Native: The café is next to the park.

Checklist

  • ☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?
  • ☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?
  • ☐ Did the lesson touch all three pillars?
  • ☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
