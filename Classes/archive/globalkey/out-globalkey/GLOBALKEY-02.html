Telling the Time and Making Plans

3-PILLAR CURRICULUM FRAMEWORK

Class: GLOBALKEY-02   Student name: ____________________   CEFR phase: Global Key (A1)

Target goal: Say and ask what time something happens using at with times and simple time expressions.

Can-do objective: Can understand and ask about times and simple timetables for everyday activities.

Balance: Skeleton timing: Warm-up 10% | Theory 25-30% | Comprehension 25-30% | Conversation 25-30% | Wrap-up 5-10%

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a clock at half past eight. Ask: What time do you start work or school?

Tutor action: Model a short answer: I start at eight thirty. Let the student answer freely.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: What time is it? It is + time. / The class starts at + time.

Examples:

  • What time is it? It is half past eight.
  • The class starts at nine o'clock.
  • The shop closes at six in the evening.

Execution method: Show the clock on screen and the question formula. Ask concept-checking questions: Do we use at before a time? What words describe half past? Then ask the student to say the time shown on the clock.

Check question: Do we say the class starts in nine or at nine?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this timetable aloud twice at a steady pace: Bus 12 leaves at 7:15. Bus 14 leaves at 8:30. The last bus leaves at 10 at night.

Direct fact check

Ask: When does Bus 12 leave? (7:15). When does the last bus leave? (10 at night).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is the timetable information urgent or routine? Accept a simple answer and ask the student to point to the word that shows timing, such as last.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Timetable question: the student asks the tutor about the bus times on screen, then they swap. The student must ask two questions using What time and reply with a time using at.

Target phrases / vocab

  • What time does ... leave?
  • It leaves at ...
  • The next one is at ...

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Use the screen timetable and praise each complete question.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • The bus leaves in eight thirty.
  • What time is the class?

Good vs. native-like upgrades

  • Accurate: The bus leaves in eight thirty.
    Native: The bus leaves at half past eight.
  • Accurate: What time is the class?
    Native: What time does the class start?

Checklist

  • ☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?
  • ☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?
  • ☐ Did the lesson touch all three pillars?
  • ☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
