Holidays and Habits: Adverbs of Frequency

3-PILLAR CURRICULUM FRAMEWORK

Class: GLOBALKEY-10   Student name: ____________________   CEFR phase: Global Key (A2)

Target goal: Describe how often you do things on holiday using always, usually, sometimes, and never.

Can-do objective: Can talk about holidays, habits, and routines using simple sentences and understand a short personal description.

Balance: Skeleton timing: Warm-up 10% | Theory 25-30% | Comprehension 25-30% | Conversation 25-30% | Wrap-up 5-10%

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a picture of a beach holiday. Ask: Do you go on holiday often?

Tutor action: Give a short example: I usually go to the beach in summer. Let the student answer freely.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Subject + adverb of frequency (always, usually, often, sometimes, never) + verb; adverb goes before the main verb but after am / is / are

Examples:

  • I always take a camera on holiday.
  • We usually visit the coast in summer.
  • She is never late for the train.

Execution method: Show the adverb scale from always to never. Ask concept-checking questions: Where does the adverb go with the verb to be? Where does it go with other verbs? Then ask the student to rank their holiday habits using two adverbs.

Check question: Where does never go in the sentence She is never late?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice at a steady pace: Mark usually goes on holiday in August. He always visits his family first. Sometimes he goes hiking, but he is never bored on the coast.

Direct fact check

Ask: When does Mark usually go on holiday? (August). What does he always do first? (visit his family). What does he sometimes do? (go hiking).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does Mark enjoy his holidays? Accept a simple answer and ask the student to point to the phrase that shows it, such as never bored.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Holiday habits interview: the student tells the tutor how often they do four things on holiday (swim, take photos, cook, relax), using adverbs of frequency. The tutor asks one follow-up question: Why do you like that?

Target phrases / vocab

  • I always ...
  • I usually ...
  • I sometimes ... but I never ...

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Use the beach picture on screen and praise each complete sentence before correcting.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • I go always to the beach.
  • She never is late.

Good vs. native-like upgrades

  • Accurate: I go always to the beach.
    Native: I always go to the beach.
  • Accurate: She never is late.
    Native: She is never late.

Checklist

  • ☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?
  • ☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?
  • ☐ Did the lesson touch all three pillars?
  • ☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
