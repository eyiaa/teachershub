Weather and Seasons: It Is Hot and It Is Cold

3-PILLAR CURRICULUM FRAMEWORK

Class: GLOBALKEY-05   Student name: ____________________   CEFR phase: Global Key (A1)

Target goal: Describe the weather and seasons using it is with simple adjectives.

Can-do objective: Can describe the weather and seasons using simple adjectives and understand a short forecast.

Balance: Skeleton timing: Warm-up 10% | Theory 25-30% | Comprehension 25-30% | Conversation 25-30% | Wrap-up 5-10%

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a picture of a sunny beach and a snowy street. Ask: Which weather do you prefer?

Tutor action: Give a short example: I prefer sunny days. Let the student answer freely.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: It is + adjective (hot, cold, rainy, windy, sunny) / In + season

Examples:

  • It is hot in summer.
  • It is cold and windy today.
  • In winter, it is often rainy.

Execution method: Show the weather adjectives with matching pictures. Ask concept-checking questions: Do we use it is for weather? Which season has snow? Then ask the student to describe today's weather using two adjectives.

Check question: Do we say the weather is hot or it weather is hot?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this forecast twice at a steady pace: Tomorrow it will be sunny in the morning. In the afternoon, it will be windy and cold. There is a chance of rain in the evening.

Direct fact check

Ask: What will the weather be in the morning? (sunny). What will the afternoon be like? (windy and cold). When is the chance of rain? (in the evening).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does the forecast sound good or bad for a day out? Accept a simple answer and ask the student to point to a word that shows it, such as chance.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Weather report: the student gives a short weather report for tomorrow to the tutor, using three adjectives, then describes their favourite season with it is.

Target phrases / vocab

  • It is sunny / cold / windy.
  • In summer, it is ...
  • I like it when it is ...

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Use the weather pictures on screen and praise each complete report.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • The weather is very hot in summer and it is good.
  • It sunny today.

Good vs. native-like upgrades

  • Accurate: The weather is very hot in summer and it is good.
    Native: It is very hot in summer, and I like it.
  • Accurate: It sunny today.
    Native: It is sunny today.

Checklist

  • ☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?
  • ☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?
  • ☐ Did the lesson touch all three pillars?
  • ☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
