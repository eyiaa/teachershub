1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Ask: Which device or app do you use most every day, and how is it kept secure?

Tutor action: Give a 15-second example: My phone is updated every month. Let the student answer freely.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Passive: object + is / was / has been + past participle. Present: The app is updated every week. Past: The app was updated yesterday. Present perfect: The file has been deleted.

Examples

  • The app is updated every week.
  • The password was changed yesterday.
  • The account has been hacked, so it has been locked.

Execution method: Show the three passive forms side by side with an active version. Ask guiding questions: Who does the action? Is the action or the doer more important? Then ask the student to change one active sentence about their phone or computer into the passive.

Check question: In The password was changed yesterday, is the focus on the action or on who changed it?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice at a steady pace: Our online account was hacked last month. The password has been changed, and the emails were checked by the security team. Two-step verification is now used for all logins.

Direct fact check

Ask: What happened to the online account? (it was hacked). What has been changed? (the password). What is now used for all logins? (two-step verification).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is the writer worried or reassured at the end? Ask the student to point to the phrase that shows the reassurance, such as is now used for all logins.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Device story: the student describes how their phone or computer is looked after (updated, backed up, protected) using at least three passive sentences. The tutor asks: Who does that for you?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • It is updated ...
  • It was ... yesterday.
  • It has been ...

Tutor mandate

Do not interrupt mid-sentence. Let the student finish the description, and note passive errors for the wrap-up.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • The app is update every week.
  • The file was delete yesterday.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did I guide the student to discover the grammar rule instead of lecturing?

☐ Did I avoid interrupting mid-sentence during conversation?

☐ Is the student speaking for at least 60-65% of the class?

☐ Did we save error correction for the dedicated feedback stage?
