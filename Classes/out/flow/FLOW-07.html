Passive Voice: How Things Are Made

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a photo of a bridge. Ask: Who do you think built this bridge, and when?

Tutor action: Give a 15-second example: This bridge was built in 1950. Let the student speak freely and note the verb forms.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Object + am / is / are (present) or was / were (past) + past participle (+ by + agent if needed)

Examples

  • Bread is made from flour and water.
  • The factory was built in 1990.
  • The letters were sent yesterday by post.

Execution method: Show the passive pattern on screen beside an active sentence. Ask guiding questions: Who does the action? Is the action or the doer more important? Let the student change one active sentence into the passive.

Check question: In The factory was built, is the focus on the builders or on the factory?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice: Coffee is grown in many tropical countries. The beans are picked by hand, then they are dried and roasted. Finally, the coffee is packed and sent to shops around the world.

Direct fact check

Ask: Where is coffee grown? (tropical countries). How are the beans picked? (by hand). What happens to the coffee at the end? (it is packed and sent to shops).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does the writer focus on the people who grow the coffee or on the process? Ask the student to point to the phrase that shows the focus, such as are picked by hand.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Process explanation: the student explains how a familiar product or meal is made using at least three passive sentences. The tutor asks one question: Who usually does this step?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • It is made from ...
  • It is cooked in ...
  • It is sold by ...

Tutor mandate

Do not interrupt mid-sentence. Let the student explain the whole process first, then note passive errors for the wrap-up.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • Bread is make from flour.
  • The factory was build in 1990.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did I guide the student to discover the grammar rule instead of lecturing?

☐ Did I avoid interrupting mid-sentence during conversation?

☐ Is the student speaking for at least 60-65% of the class?

☐ Did we save error correction for the dedicated feedback stage?
