1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Ask: What would you want to know about a company before you applied for a job there?

Tutor action: Give one example question, then ask the student to add a second.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Could you tell me + question word + subject + verb? / I was wondering whether + subject + verb / Do you mind telling me + question word + clause?

Examples

  • Could you tell me what a typical day in this role looks like?
  • I was wondering whether there are training opportunities in the first year.
  • Do you mind telling me how success is measured in this team?

Execution method: Show direct questions beside the polite indirect versions. Ask guiding questions: Which version is more polite? Does the word order change after the question word? Then ask the student to turn one direct question into a polite indirect question.

Check question: In Could you tell me what a typical day looks like, is the word order the same as in a direct question?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this short dialogue twice at a steady pace: Interviewer: Do you have any questions for us? Candidate: Yes. Could you tell me how the team works together? I was wondering whether there are training opportunities in the first year.

Direct fact check

Ask: What does the candidate ask about the team? (how the team works together). What else do they ask about? (training opportunities in the first year).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is the candidate polite or demanding? Ask the student to point to the phrase that shows politeness, such as I was wondering whether.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Question round: the tutor plays the interviewer, who ends with: Do you have any questions for us? The student asks three polite questions about the role, the team, and development. The tutor answers one of them.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • Could you tell me ...?
  • I was wondering whether ...
  • Do you mind telling me ...?

Tutor mandate

Do not interrupt mid-sentence. Let the student ask each question fully, and note word order errors for the wrap-up.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • Could you tell me what is a typical day?
  • I want to know do you have training.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did I guide the student to discover the grammar rule instead of lecturing?

☐ Did I avoid interrupting mid-sentence during conversation?

☐ Is the student speaking for at least 60-65% of the class?

☐ Did we save error correction for the dedicated feedback stage?
