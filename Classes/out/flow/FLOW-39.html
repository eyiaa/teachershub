1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Ask: Should students learn a second language at school? Give one reason for your answer.

Tutor action: Let the student give a one-sentence opinion, then ask for one more reason with because.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Opinion: In my view, ... / Reason: Moreover, ... / Contrast: However, ... / Although ..., ... / Despite ..., ... / Conclusion: Therefore, ...

Examples

  • In my view, learning a language is worth the effort. Moreover, it helps people understand other cultures.
  • On the other hand, it takes a lot of time. However, the benefits last for years.
  • Although the course is expensive, it is worth it. Therefore, I would recommend it.

Execution method: Show the paragraph with each linking word labelled by its job: opinion, reason, contrast, conclusion. Ask guiding questions: Does this word add an idea, show a contrast, or lead to a result? Then ask the student to write a three-sentence paragraph on the warm-up question using one linking word from each job.

Check question: Does therefore introduce a reason or a result?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this paragraph twice at a steady pace: Public transport should be free. Moreover, it would reduce traffic in the city. On the other hand, it would cost the council a lot. However, the long-term savings could be large. Therefore, the council should at least consider it.

Direct fact check

Ask: What is the writer's opinion? (public transport should be free). What would free transport reduce? (traffic). What would it cost? (a lot for the council). What does the writer conclude? (the council should consider it).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is the writer certain about the idea? Ask the student to point to the linking word that shows the writer considers both sides, such as On the other hand.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Paragraph talk: the student gives a short opinion on the warm-up question to the tutor, with two reasons and one counter-argument, using at least three linking words. The tutor asks: What would you conclude?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • In my view, ...
  • However, ...
  • Therefore, ...

Tutor mandate

Do not interrupt mid-sentence. Let the student give the full opinion, and note linking-word errors for the wrap-up.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • Although it is expensive, but it is worth it.
  • It is good, therefore, because it is good for the people.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did I guide the student to discover the grammar rule instead of lecturing?

☐ Did I avoid interrupting mid-sentence during conversation?

☐ Is the student speaking for at least 60-65% of the class?

☐ Did we save error correction for the dedicated feedback stage?
