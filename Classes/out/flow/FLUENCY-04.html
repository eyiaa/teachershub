1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Ask: Should children learn a second language at school?

Tutor action: Set a 60-second timer and start it when the student begins.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: I believe + opinion, because + reason. For example, + example. However, + counter-point (optional).

Examples

  • I believe public transport should be cheaper, because it helps people who do not have cars.
  • For example, our city's bus fare doubled last year.
  • However, some people think the budget is better spent on roads.

Execution method: Show the frame with a timer. Ask guiding questions: What is your opinion? What is the reason? What is the example? Then ask the student to practise the frame in 60 seconds on a short topic.

Check question: Which part of the frame gives a real example?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Listen to or read this answer twice at a steady pace: I think remote work is good for most people, because it saves travel time. For example, my colleague now spends two more hours a day with her family. However, some people feel isolated, so companies need to support them.

Direct fact check

Ask: What does the speaker think about remote work? (it is good for most people). What does a colleague gain? (two more hours a day with family). What do some people feel? (isolated).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does the speaker agree fully with remote work? Ask the student to point to the phrase that shows balance, such as However.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Sixty-second opinion: the tutor gives a topic card (remote work, online shopping, city living). The student speaks for 60 seconds using the frame and one linking word. Then repeat once with a new topic and aim to be faster and smoother.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • I believe ..., because ...
  • For example, ...
  • However, ...

Tutor mandate

Keep time visibly. Do not interrupt during the 60 seconds. After each turn, note one fluency gain and one priority for the next attempt.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • I think it is good because is good for people, and example my city is bad.
  • However some people is alone, so company must help.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did I guide the student to discover the grammar rule instead of lecturing?

☐ Did I avoid interrupting mid-sentence during conversation?

☐ Is the student speaking for at least 60-65% of the class?

☐ Did we save error correction for the dedicated feedback stage?
