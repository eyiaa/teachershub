1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Ask: What did your manager or teacher say to you last week? Give a 15-second example: My teacher said that the test would be on Monday.

Tutor action: Let the student speak freely for 1 to 2 minutes and note any reported speech for later.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Subject + said / told + (that) + statement with the tense moved back one step (is becomes was; will becomes would)

Examples

  • She said, I am tired. She said that she was tired.
  • He said, I will call you. He said he would call me.
  • They asked, Where do you live? They asked where I lived.

Execution method: Show direct speech on the left and reported speech on the right. Ask guiding questions: What changed in the tense? What changed in the pronoun? Let the student report one thing a friend said.

Check question: When we report a question, do we keep the question word order or change it?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice: Yesterday my manager told us that the meeting had moved to Friday. She also said that the new software would arrive next week and asked us to read the manual.

Direct fact check

Ask: When is the meeting now? (Friday). When will the software arrive? (next week). What did the manager ask them to do? (read the manual).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is the manager's tone urgent or relaxed? Ask the student to point to the phrase that shows the tone, such as asked us to read.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Relay game: the tutor says a short statement or question. The student reports it to a third person (for example, to a colleague). Then the student reports one thing the tutor said earlier in the lesson.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • He said that ...
  • She told me ...
  • They asked me ...

Tutor mandate

Do not interrupt mid-sentence. Let the student finish the report before correcting, and keep tense errors for the wrap-up.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • She said that she is tired.
  • He asked me where do I live.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did I guide the student to discover the grammar rule instead of lecturing?

☐ Did I avoid interrupting mid-sentence during conversation?

☐ Is the student speaking for at least 60-65% of the class?

☐ Did we save error correction for the dedicated feedback stage?
