1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Read aloud: I want to go to the market tomorrow. Ask: Which words did you stress?

Tutor action: Model the sentence once with natural stress, then ask the student to say it with you.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Content words (nouns, main verbs, adjectives) carry stress. Function words (to, and, can, of) are weak. Weak forms link the sentence together.

Examples

  • We need to finish the REPORT by FRIDAY.
  • Do you want a COFFEE?
  • I want to go to the MARKET.

Execution method: Play or read each sentence twice: first slowly with full forms, then at natural speed. Ask guiding questions: Which words stand out? Which words sound weaker? Then ask the student to read one sentence with the stressed words in capitals.

Check question: In We need to finish the REPORT by FRIDAY, which word carries the most stress?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this line twice at natural speed, then once slowly: I wanna get the report finished by Friday, so I'm gonna need some help from the team.

Direct fact check

Ask: What does the speaker need to get finished? (the report). By when? (Friday). Who might help? (the team).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does the speaker sound urgent or relaxed? Ask the student to point to the words that sound fast and weak, such as wanna and gonna.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Read-aloud challenge: the student reads three short sentences from the tutor's card with natural stress and linking. Then the student answers one question about the topic, keeping the same rhythm.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • I want to ...
  • We need to ... by ...
  • Do you want ...?

Tutor mandate

Model the rhythm first, then let the student copy it. Do not correct every sound. Focus on stress on content words and a steady flow.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • I want go to the shop tomorrow.
  • We need finish the report by Friday.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did I guide the student to discover the grammar rule instead of lecturing?

☐ Did I avoid interrupting mid-sentence during conversation?

☐ Is the student speaking for at least 60-65% of the class?

☐ Did we save error correction for the dedicated feedback stage?
