1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Ask: What is one thing you are good at, and one thing you are working on improving?

Tutor action: Give a short example, then let the student speak for about one minute.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: I'm good at + -ing / I'm interested in + -ing / I'm working on + -ing (an area I am improving)

Examples

  • I'm good at explaining complex ideas to clients.
  • I'm interested in working with international teams.
  • I'm working on speaking more confidently in meetings.

Execution method: Show the three frames with examples. Ask guiding questions: What form follows at, in, and on? Is the weakness presented as something being improved? Then ask the student to finish the three frames about themselves.

Check question: After I'm good at, do we use a verb in the base form or the -ing form?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this interview answer twice at a steady pace: I'm good at organising projects, and I'm interested in leading larger teams. One thing I'm working on is delegating more. I used to do everything myself, but now I'm learning to trust my colleagues.

Direct fact check

Ask: What is the speaker good at? (organising projects). What are they interested in? (leading larger teams). What are they working on? (delegating more).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does the speaker sound honest and self-aware? Ask the student to point to the phrase that shows self-awareness, such as I used to do everything myself.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Honest self-assessment: the tutor asks: What are your main strengths, and what are you working on? The student gives at least two strengths and one weakness, framed as an area of improvement.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • I'm good at ...
  • I'm interested in ...
  • I'm working on ...

Tutor mandate

Do not interrupt mid-sentence. Let the student speak fully, and suggest one stronger phrase in the chat only after the answer is complete.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • I am good in organise projects.
  • One weakness is I am very lazy sometimes.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did I guide the student to discover the grammar rule instead of lecturing?

☐ Did I avoid interrupting mid-sentence during conversation?

☐ Is the student speaking for at least 60-65% of the class?

☐ Did we save error correction for the dedicated feedback stage?
