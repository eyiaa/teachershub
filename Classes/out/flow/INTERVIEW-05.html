1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Ask: If you were interviewing for your dream job, what would make you most nervous?

Tutor action: Let the student answer, then ask what they would do to feel calmer.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: If I got the job, I would + base verb (a plan for the future) / I have + past participle (experience so far) / I think that would + base verb (a reason)

Examples

  • If I got this role, I would focus on building client relationships.
  • I have worked in sales, so I would bring that experience to the team.
  • I think that would improve the service for our customers.

Execution method: Show the three frames with examples. Ask guiding questions: Is the plan real or hypothetical? Which tense describes experience so far? Then ask the student to give one answer about the job they want.

Check question: In If I got this role, I would focus on clients, is the situation real or hypothetical?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this interview answer twice at a steady pace: I have worked in retail for three years, so I know how to deal with difficult customers. If I got this position, I would introduce a regular feedback session for the team, because I think that would improve service.

Direct fact check

Ask: How long has the candidate worked in retail? (three years). What would they introduce if they got the job? (a regular feedback session).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does the candidate sound confident about the plan? Ask the student to point to the phrase that gives the reason, such as because I think that would improve service.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Full mock interview: the tutor plays the interviewer and asks four questions: introduce yourself, describe a past achievement, name a weakness, and explain what you would do in this role. The student answers each in one to two minutes. The tutor then gives one piece of feedback.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • If I got this role, I would ...
  • I have worked ... for ...
  • I think that would ...

Tutor mandate

Act as a formal interviewer. Keep the tone professional, let the student answer each question fully, and note errors silently for the feedback.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • If I will get the job, I would be happy.
  • I work in sales for five years and I know it well.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did I guide the student to discover the grammar rule instead of lecturing?

☐ Did I avoid interrupting mid-sentence during conversation?

☐ Is the student speaking for at least 60-65% of the class?

☐ Did we save error correction for the dedicated feedback stage?
