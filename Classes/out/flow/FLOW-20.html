1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a photo of a dark cloud over a beach. Ask: What is going to happen, and what are you going to do about it?

Tutor action: Give a 15-second example: It is going to rain, so I will take an umbrella. Let the student answer freely.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Will + base verb (spontaneous decision or prediction without evidence); going to + base verb (plan or prediction with evidence now); present continuous for fixed arrangements (I am meeting her at six)

Examples

  • I am tired, so I will go to bed early. (decision at the moment)
  • Look at those clouds. It is going to rain. (evidence now)
  • We are flying to Madrid on Friday. (fixed arrangement)

Execution method: Show the three examples. Ask guiding questions: Was the decision made before or at the moment of speaking? Is there evidence now? Is there a fixed time or booking? Then ask the student to make one sentence for each form about their plans this week.

Check question: In I am meeting her at six, is the arrangement fixed or a spontaneous idea?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice at a steady pace: Next month, Sofia is going to start a new course. She is starting on Monday, and she will need to buy books first. She thinks the course will be difficult, but she is sure it will help her career.

Direct fact check

Ask: What is Sofia going to start? (a new course). When does she start? (on Monday). What will she need to buy? (books).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is Sofia excited or nervous about the course? Ask the student to point to the phrase that shows her feeling, such as she is sure it will help.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: My week: the student describes three things they will do or are doing next week, using one of each future form. The tutor asks one question: Why did you decide to do that?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • I will ... because ...
  • I am going to ...
  • I am ...-ing on ...

Tutor mandate

Do not interrupt mid-sentence. Let the student finish each plan, and note future form errors for the wrap-up.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • I will meet my friend on Friday, I decided it yesterday.
  • I am going to meet my doctor at ten tomorrow.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did I guide the student to discover the grammar rule instead of lecturing?

☐ Did I avoid interrupting mid-sentence during conversation?

☐ Is the student speaking for at least 60-65% of the class?

☐ Did we save error correction for the dedicated feedback stage?
