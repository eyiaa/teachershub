Describing Changes and Goals: Used To and Future Plans

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Review: What do you do on Sundays? Then ask: What did you use to do as a child? Show a photo of a child playing outside.

Tutor action: Accept any answer, even a short one, and expand it: You used to play outside. Then ask one question about the present.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Used to + base verb: a past habit or state that is no longer true (I used to take the bus). Negative: didn't use to (I didn't use to like coffee). Question: Did you use to ...? Now + present simple (Now I walk to work.). Future intention: going to + base verb (I'm going to study more next month.).

Examples

  • I used to take the bus, but now I walk.
  • She used to be shy, but now she speaks in public.
  • I didn't use to like coffee. Now I drink it every day.
  • I'm going to study more next month.

Execution method: Show a before-and-after picture: a person with a bike, then a person with a car. Say I used to ride a bike. Now I drive a car. Make the contrast clear: used to for past habits and the present simple for now. Show that the question form is Did you use to ...?, with no -ed ending. The student completes: I ___ (use to) live in a small town. Now I ___ (live) in the city. Then the student names one goal with I'm going to.

Check question: Do we say I used to plays or I used to play?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice, slowly: Hi, I'm Carlos. I used to be very shy. I didn't use to speak English in class. Now I speak with my friends every day, and I'm not afraid of mistakes. I'm going to take an English exam next year, and I'm going to practise every morning. I also want to read more books.

Direct fact check

Ask: What was Carlos like before? (very shy). Did he speak English in class before? (No). What does he do now with his friends? (speaks English every day). What is he going to do next year? (take an English exam).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is Carlos more confident now? Which words show it? (I'm not afraid of mistakes).

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Then and now: the student describes two changes in their life, things they used to do and do now, and two goals for the next year. The tutor asks one follow-up question for each change and goal, such as What made you change?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • I used to ..., but now I ...
  • I didn't use to ...
  • Now I ... every day.
  • I'm going to ... next year.
  • What made you change?

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Use the student's real changes, or accept invented ones if the student prefers. Note whether the student describes two changes and two goals, and mark accuracy on a 1 to 4 scale.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • I used to takes the bus.
  • I didn't used to like coffee.
  • I'm going study English next year.
