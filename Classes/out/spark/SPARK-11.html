Weather and Seasons: What's the Weather Like?

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Review: Is there a park near your home? How do you get there? Then ask: What's the weather like today? Do you want to go outside?

Tutor action: Link outdoor places from the last class to the weather. Accept one word, such as sunny, and expand it: It's sunny today.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: It's + sunny / cloudy / windy / hot / warm / cool / cold. It's raining. It's snowing. + In summer, it's often hot. + When it's cold, I wear a jacket. / I stay at home because it's raining.

Examples

  • It's sunny and warm today.
  • When it's raining, I wear a raincoat.
  • In winter, it's usually cold in my city.
  • I go to the beach because it's hot.

Execution method: Show weather symbols for sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy, and snowy, and the temperature words hot, warm, cool, and cold. Practise What's the weather like? and Is it ...? Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. Show the four seasons, and note that some countries have a rainy season and a dry season instead. Then match weather to clothes: hot and sunny, a T-shirt and a hat; cold, a jacket and a sweater; rainy, a raincoat.

Check question: Do we say It's rain or It's raining?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this weather forecast twice, slowly: Good morning! Here is the weather for Saturday. In London, it's cloudy and cool, 14 degrees. In Madrid, it's sunny and hot, 30 degrees. In Toronto, it's raining and cold, 8 degrees. Take an umbrella!

Direct fact check

Ask: What's the weather like in Madrid? (sunny and hot). What's the temperature in Toronto? (8 degrees). Where do you need an umbrella? (Toronto).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Which city is good for the beach? Why? Accept a short reason with because.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Let's make weekend plans: the student and tutor look at the forecast and choose a city for the weekend. The student describes the weather in two cities, chooses clothes, and suggests one activity with a reason. Then the tutor gives a short spoken forecast for a new city, and the student says what the weather is and picks an activity.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • What's the weather like in ...?
  • It's sunny / cloudy / raining / cold.
  • In summer / In winter, it's usually ...
  • When it's ..., I wear ...
  • I want to ... because it's ...

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Respect different climates and seasons. Let the student choose, and ask Why? to get a reason with because.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • Today is sun.
  • It's rain in Toronto.
  • When is cold, I wear jacket.
