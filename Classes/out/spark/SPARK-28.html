Preferences and Reasons: Like, Love, Hate, and Would Rather

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Review: What did you do last weekend? Then ask: What do you enjoy doing in your free time? Show four pictures: a movie, hiking, cooking, and a concert.

Tutor action: Accept one-word answers, such as football, and build them into a sentence: I enjoy playing football.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Like / love / enjoy / hate + verb-ing (I like cooking. She hates getting up early.). Would rather + base verb + than (I'd rather stay home than go out.). Reason: because + reason (I like hiking because it helps me relax.). Result: so + result (It was raining, so we stayed home.)

Examples

  • I enjoy hiking because it helps me relax.
  • I'd rather stay home tonight.
  • I hate getting up early, so I go to bed early.
  • She loves cooking because it's creative.

Execution method: Show four activity pictures. Model I love ... with -ing. Explain that like, love, enjoy, and hate are followed by -ing. Show that would rather is followed by a base verb: I'd rather watch a movie. Show that because gives a reason and so gives a result. The student completes: I enjoy ___ (cook). I'd rather ___ (stay) at home than ___ (go) out. I like swimming because ___.

Check question: After enjoy, do we say enjoy to read or enjoy reading?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice, slowly: Hi, I'm Sofia. I love music because it makes me happy. I enjoy going to concerts, but I hate queuing for tickets. This weekend, I'd rather stay home and listen to music than go to a big concert. My brother prefers football, so we often watch matches together on Saturday.

Direct fact check

Ask: Why does Sofia love music? (it makes her happy). What does she hate? (queuing for tickets). What would she rather do this weekend? (stay home and listen to music). What does her brother prefer? (football).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does Sofia enjoy concerts? Which words show it? (I enjoy going to concerts, but I hate queuing).

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Choose an activity together: the student and tutor choose a weekend activity from four options. The student says what they like, love, and hate about each option, with a reason using because. The student says which one they would rather do than the others. The tutor disagrees politely, and they agree on one activity.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • I love / like / enjoy ... -ing
  • I hate ... -ing
  • I'd rather ... than ...
  • ... because ...
  • I'm not sure. I'd rather ...

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Disagree politely so the student has a reason to justify a choice. Count the preferences the student supports with a reason, target three, and mark task completion and accuracy on a 1 to 4 scale.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • I like to swimming.
  • I'd rather to stay home.
  • I love cook because is fun.
