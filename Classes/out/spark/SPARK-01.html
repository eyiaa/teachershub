My First English Words: Letters, Numbers, and Classroom English

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a slide with Hello, Good morning, How are you?, Nice to meet you, and Goodbye. Say: Hello! My name is Anna. Ask: What is your name?

Tutor action: Greet the student with a smile and gestures. Model each greeting once, then let the student say hello and their name. Note what English the student already knows.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Alphabet A to Z (vowels A, E, I, O, U) + numbers 0 to 20, then tens to 100 (21 = twenty-one) + My name is ... / I am ... years old. / I am from ...

Examples

  • My name is Sara. S-A-R-A.
  • I am twenty-five years old.
  • I am from Mexico. My birthday is in May.

Execution method: Go through the alphabet in groups of five letters. The student repeats, then spells their first name. Count 0 to 20 together, count by tens to 100, and build three numbers such as 21, 35, and 48. Show colours and shapes, objects (book, pen, phone), the days of the week, and the months on one slide each, and ask the student to point and say the word. Teach six classroom words: listen, repeat, read, write, look, point. Finish with the three personal sentences.

Check question: How do you spell your name? How old are you? Which month is your birthday?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this short text twice, slowly: Hello! My name is Tom. T-O-M. I am thirty years old. I am from Canada. My birthday is in May. My favorite color is blue.

Direct fact check

Ask: What is his name? (Tom). How do you spell it? (T-O-M). How old is he? (thirty). Where is he from? (Canada). When is his birthday? (May). Accept one-word answers.

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is Tom friendly? Accept a gesture, a smile, or one word such as yes or happy.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: First English challenge: the tutor gives quick prompts and the student answers. Spell your name. Say five numbers the tutor writes. Name three colours on screen. Say today's day and your birthday month. Follow three classroom instructions: listen, repeat, point. Then introduce yourself with hello, your name, your age, and your country.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • Hello! My name is ...
  • I am ... years old.
  • I am from ...
  • Can you repeat, please?
  • I don't understand.

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Use gestures and pictures instead of translation. Praise every attempt, and note which tasks the student did alone, with help, or not yet, to see which basics need more practice.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • My name Sara.
  • I have twenty-five years.
  • My birthday is on May.

Tutor checklist

☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?

☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?

☐ Am I speaking at a steady, clear pace without complex idioms?

☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
