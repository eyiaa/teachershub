My Daily Routine: Present Simple and Telling the Time

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Ask: Tell me about a family member or friend. Then ask: What do you do every day? Give your own example: I work every day.

Tutor action: Accept one word or a short phrase. Say it back as a full sentence without explaining the grammar.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: I + verb + at + time / in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening / at night. What time do you ...? Do you ...? Yes, I do. / No, I don't. Sequence words: First, Then, Next, Finally.

Examples

  • I wake up at six o'clock.
  • I have breakfast at seven thirty.
  • First, I take a shower. Then, I have breakfast.
  • Do you work in the afternoon? Yes, I do.

Execution method: Show pictures of a day: wake up, take a shower, get dressed, have breakfast, have lunch, work or study, go home, have dinner, go to bed. Show four clock times: 6:00 six o'clock, 7:30 seven thirty, 12:00 twelve o'clock, 9:15 nine fifteen. The student completes sentences with their own times. Use only I and you, and do not teach he or she with -s yet.

Check question: Do we say I wake up at six or I wake up in six?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice, slowly: My name is Leo. I wake up at six o'clock. First, I take a shower. Then, I have breakfast at seven. I work in the morning. I have lunch at one o'clock. In the evening, I have dinner and watch TV. I go to bed at ten thirty.

Direct fact check

Ask: What time does Leo wake up? (six o'clock). When does he have lunch? (one o'clock). What does he do in the evening? (has dinner and watches TV). Accept short answers.

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is Leo's day busy or relaxed? Accept a simple answer and one reason.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: A day in my life: the student tells the tutor about a typical day, with at least five activities, two times, and two sequence words. Then the student asks the tutor three questions, such as: What time do you wake up? Do you study every day? What do you do in the evening?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • I wake up at ...
  • I have breakfast / lunch / dinner at ...
  • First, ... Then, ... Next, ... Finally, ...
  • What time do you ...?
  • Do you ...? Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Let the student describe their real routine in their own order. Do not interrupt for small errors; note them for the wrap-up.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • I wake up in six o'clock.
  • I go to the bed at ten.
  • You study every day? Yes, I study.

Tutor checklist
