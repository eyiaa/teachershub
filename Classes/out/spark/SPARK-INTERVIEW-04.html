1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Ask: What would you like to know about a job before you start it?

Tutor action: Give a short example: What time does the job start? Let the student answer and ask one more question.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Do you + verb ...? (yes or no) / What is + noun ...? (information) / Could you repeat that, please? (asking again)

Examples

  • Do you have experience with computers?
  • What is the salary for this job?
  • Could you repeat that, please?

Execution method: Show the three question types with examples. Ask guiding questions: Which question needs yes or no? Which one asks for information? Then ask the student to write one question for an interview.

Check question: Does Do you have experience ask for a yes or no answer, or for information?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this short interview dialogue twice at a steady pace: Interviewer: Do you have experience in sales? Candidate: Yes, I do. What is the working time? Interviewer: Nine to five. Candidate: Could you repeat that, please?

Direct fact check

Ask: Does the candidate have sales experience? (yes). What is the working time? (nine to five). What does the candidate ask for? (to repeat it).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is the candidate polite? Ask the student to point to the polite phrase, such as Could you repeat that, please?

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Mini mock interview: the tutor plays the interviewer and asks: Do you have experience? What is your job now? Do you have any questions? The student answers and asks at least one question, using Could you repeat or What is.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • Yes, I do.
  • What is ...?
  • Could you repeat that, please?

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Speak slowly, let the student answer each question, and praise each polite question.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • Do you have experience? Yes I have.
  • What is the time work?

Tutor checklist

☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?

☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?

☐ Am I speaking at a steady, clear pace without complex idioms?

☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
