1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Review: Tell me about something you did recently. What are you doing this week? Which is better for you, your city or a city you visited?

Tutor action: Keep the warm-up short. Accept a full answer, and give one brief compliment on something the student said accurately.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Review the A2 structures: past simple (I visited), past continuous (I was cooking when the phone rang), present perfect (Have you ever ...?), going to and will (I'm going to ... / I think it will ...), comparatives (bigger than), and modals (should, have to, could).

Examples

  • Last month I visited a new city. It was quieter than mine.
  • Next year I'm going to return.
  • Have you ever been to Lisbon? Yes, I have. I went there two years ago.
  • I used to be shy, but now I speak with my friends.

Execution method: Use one slide per structure. Show each one with one example and one question, and ask the student to produce a sentence for each without notes. Then mix the structures: ask a question that needs two tenses, such as What did you do last weekend, and what are you going to do next weekend? Score the performance with the rubric for task completion, fluency, accuracy, comprehension, and writing. Do not stop the student for small errors.

Check question: For a decision made at the moment of speaking, do we use going to or will?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this message twice, slowly: Hi Mia, last month I visited Porto with my brother. It was a lot of fun. We walked along the river, and we ate fish every day. The city was smaller than I expected, but the food was better than in my town. Next year, I'm going to go back in the summer. Have you ever been there? You should come with us!

Direct fact check

Ask: When did the writer visit Porto? (last month). Who went with him? (his brother). What did they do along the river? (walked). What is the writer going to do next year? (go back in the summer).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does the writer like Porto? Which words show it? (It was a lot of fun, the food was better).

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Integrated interview and message: the tutor interviews the student for about five minutes. The tutor asks about a past experience, a comparison of two places, and a future plan. Then the student writes a 70 to 90 word message to a friend about the same topics, using at least two linking words such as because, so, when, or while.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • Last ..., I ...
  • ... is more ... than ...
  • I'm going to ... next ...
  • I think it will ...
  • Have you ever ...?

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Run the interview as a real conversation, with follow-up questions. This is the final performance assessment for the A2 module. Score task completion, fluency, accuracy, comprehension, and writing on a 1 to 4 scale, note one strength and one recurring gap, and set one practice goal for the student.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • Last month I visit a new city.
  • It was more quiet than mine.
  • Next year I going to return.

Tutor checklist

☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?

☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?

☐ Am I speaking at a steady, clear pace without complex idioms?

☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
