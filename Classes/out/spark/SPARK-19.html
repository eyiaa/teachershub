Past Questions and Negatives: Asking About a Trip

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Review: Last weekend, I visited my cousins. Then ask: Did you have a nice weekend? Where did you go?

Tutor action: Use Did you ...? as the model for the lesson. Accept a yes or no answer, then ask one follow-up question with What or Where.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Did + subject + base verb (Did you go? Did she like it?). Question word + did + subject + base verb (Where did you stay? What did you eat?). Negative: subject + didn't + base verb (I didn't travel. She didn't like it). Short answers: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

Examples

  • Did you travel in July?
  • Where did you stay?
  • I didn't travel in July.
  • Did you enjoy the trip? Yes, I did.

Execution method: Show the question pattern as three boxes on screen: Did / subject / base verb. Point out that the verb stays in its base form after did. Compare She went with She didn't go, and with Did she go? Show a question-word chart: who, what, where, when. Ask the student about a past trip, then give your own answer with a negative, such as I didn't go to the beach. The student makes two negatives about their last holiday.

Check question: Do we say Did you went or Did you go?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this interview twice, slowly: Interviewer: Did you go on holiday last year? Maria: Yes, I did. I went to Portugal in August. Interviewer: Where did you stay? Maria: I stayed in a small hotel near the beach. Interviewer: Did you enjoy it? Maria: Yes, I did! But I didn't go swimming. I was too tired.

Direct fact check

Ask: Where did Maria go? (Portugal). When did she go? (in August). Where did she stay? (a small hotel near the beach). Did she go swimming? (No, she didn't).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Did Maria enjoy her holiday? Which words show it? (Yes, I did! and near the beach).

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Interview the tutor: the student asks five past simple questions about a trip, such as Where did you go?, Did you like it?, and Who did you travel with? The tutor answers briefly and includes one sentence with didn't. The student asks one follow-up question after each answer.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • Did you ...?
  • Where did you ...? / What did you ...?
  • Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
  • I didn't ...
  • How long did you stay?

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Give short, clear answers so the student can ask the next question. Count the accurate follow-up questions the student asks, target five, and mark interaction and accuracy on a 1 to 4 scale.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • Did you went to Spain?
  • I didn't went to the beach.
  • Where you stayed?

Tutor checklist

☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?

☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?

☐ Am I speaking at a steady, clear pace without complex idioms?

☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
