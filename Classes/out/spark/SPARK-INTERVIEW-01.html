1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a photo of a smiling person with a name badge. Ask: What is your name, and what is your job?

Tutor action: Greet the student, state the goal on screen, and give a short example of your own answer. Let the student answer freely.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: I am + name / I am a + job / I work at + place

Examples

  • I am Maria. I am a nurse.
  • I work at a hospital in Lima.
  • He is Tom. He is an engineer at a bank.

Execution method: Show the three sentence patterns on screen with the job word highlighted. Ask guiding questions: Which word comes after I am for a job? Which word is used before the place? Then ask the student to say their own name, job, and workplace.

Check question: Do we say I am a teacher or I am teacher?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this short introduction twice at a steady pace: Hello, I am Lena. I am an accountant. I work at a small company in Madrid.

Direct fact check

Ask: What is Lena's name? (Lena). What is her job? (an accountant). Where does she work? (a small company in Madrid).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is Lena friendly or formal? Accept a simple answer and ask the student to point to the word Hello, which shows a friendly start.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Meeting a new colleague: the student introduces themselves to the tutor, who plays a new colleague at work. Then they swap roles. Use the screen prompts: What is your name? What is your job? Where do you work?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • My name is ...
  • I am a ...
  • I work at ...

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Use the screen template, and praise every complete sentence before correcting anything.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • I is Maria.
  • I work in a hospital, I am nurse.

Tutor checklist

☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?

☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?

☐ Am I speaking at a steady, clear pace without complex idioms?

☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
