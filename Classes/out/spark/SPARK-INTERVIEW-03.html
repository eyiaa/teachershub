Job Interviews 3: Talking About Past Work

1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Ask: What was your first job, or your first part-time job?

Tutor action: Give a short example: My first job was in a café. I made coffee. Let the student answer, even briefly.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Subject + past simple verb (worked, studied, went, had). Negative: did not + base verb.

Examples

  • I worked in a café for two years.
  • I studied accounting at college.
  • I did not have much experience at first.

Execution method: Show the regular and irregular verbs in two columns. Ask guiding questions: Does this verb end in ed? Is this a finished time? Then ask the student to say one past job and one past course.

Check question: Do we say I did not work or I did not worked?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice at a steady pace: Anna studied English at college. She worked in a hotel for a year. Then she went to London, and she had a job in an office there.

Direct fact check

Ask: What did Anna study? (English). Where did she work for a year? (a hotel). Where did she go? (London).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is Anna's story positive or negative? Accept a simple answer and ask the student to point to a word that shows it, such as had a job.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: My work history: the student tells the tutor about one past job and one past course, using at least three past simple verbs. The tutor asks one question: What did you learn there?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • I worked as ...
  • I studied ...
  • I did not ...

Tutor mandate

Keep your talk time under 40 percent. Give the student time to recall past verbs, and praise each complete sentence.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • I work in a hotel last year.
  • I went to college and I study accounting.

Tutor checklist

☐ Are sentence structures clearly visible on screen?

☐ Is Teacher Talk Time (TTT) under 40% of the lesson?

☐ Am I speaking at a steady, clear pace without complex idioms?

☐ Did the student speak using complete target sentences by the end?
