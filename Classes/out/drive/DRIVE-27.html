1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a photo of a car being repaired in a garage. Ask: Who would you ask to fix something like this, and why?

Tutor action: Give a short example: I have my car serviced every spring. Let the student answer and ask one question about who does the work.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: have + object + past participle (someone else does it for us); have + someone + base verb (we arrange for someone to do it); get + someone + to + verb (we persuade or arrange it with effort)

Examples

  • We had the office repainted last month.
  • She had her manager check the figures.
  • I got the supplier to deliver two days early.

Execution method: Show each causative with the agent and the action. Ask guiding questions: Who does the action? Did the speaker arrange it or persuade someone? Then ask the student to describe one thing they had or got done recently.

Check question: In I got the supplier to deliver early, did the speaker simply arrange it, or did they have to persuade someone?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice at native speed: After the flood, the council had the roads repaired within a week. The mayor got the regional government to release emergency funds, and she had local volunteers check every damaged house.

Direct fact check

Ask: What did the council have repaired? (the roads). How quickly? (within a week). Who did the mayor get to release funds? (the regional government).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does the writer present the mayor's work as effortful or routine? Ask the student to point to the phrase that shows effort, such as got the regional government to.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Organising an event: the student describes how they would organise a community event, using at least one of each causative structure. The tutor asks: Who would actually do the work, and who would you need to persuade?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • We would have ... done ...
  • I would get ... to ...
  • We had ... arranged ...

Tutor mandate

Act as a conversation partner and event partner. Let the student describe the arrangements in full, and share one more precise causative phrase live in the chat when they use a plain verb.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • We had the room clean by the staff.
  • I had the manager to check the figures.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did the student defend a stance without translating from their native language?

☐ Did I share better vocabulary live while the student spoke?

☐ Is the student talking for 75-85% of the class?

☐ Did the wrap-up review good vs. better word choices?
