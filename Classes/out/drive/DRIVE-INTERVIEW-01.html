1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Ask: Think of your most valuable professional contribution. What was the impact, in numbers if possible?

Tutor action: Give a brief example with a measurable result, then challenge the student to state the impact in one sentence.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Subject + have / has been + verb-ing (ongoing experience) + relative clause (who / which / that) giving the result

Examples

  • I have been leading the regional sales team for three years, which has increased revenue by a fifth.
  • She has been managing client accounts that generated most of the company's growth last year.
  • We have been working on a process that has cut delivery times in half.

Execution method: Show the example sentences with the ongoing activity and the relative clause highlighted. Ask guiding questions: Is the activity still continuing? What does the relative clause add? Then ask the student to rewrite one of their achievements with present perfect continuous.

Check question: In I have been leading the team, is the activity finished or still happening?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this interview answer twice at native speed: For the past four years, I have been overseeing supplier relationships, which has reduced procurement costs by about twelve percent. Most of that saving came from renegotiating contracts that had been expensive for a decade.

Direct fact check

Ask: How long has the candidate been overseeing supplier relationships? (four years). What has this reduced? (procurement costs, by about twelve percent). What was the source of most of the saving? (renegotiating expensive contracts).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does the candidate sound confident or cautious about the figure? Ask the student to point to the word that shows the figure is an estimate, such as about.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Achievement pitch: the tutor plays a hiring manager and asks: Walk me through your most significant contribution in your current role. The student gives a two-minute answer using present perfect continuous for ongoing work, a relative clause for the result, and one figure. The tutor then challenges the claim: How did you measure that?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • I have been ... for ...
  • which has ...
  • That result came from ...

Tutor mandate

Act as a coach and a sceptical hiring manager. Challenge the student to justify each claim, and share one more precise phrase in the chat when a result is stated vaguely.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • I am working here for three years, and it saves money.
  • I have been managing the team, which I did a big change.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did the student defend a stance without translating from their native language?

☐ Did I share better vocabulary live while the student spoke?

☐ Is the student talking for 75-85% of the class?

☐ Did the wrap-up review good vs. better word choices?
