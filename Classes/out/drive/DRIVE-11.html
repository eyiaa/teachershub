1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show this casual sentence on screen: We need to do something about the delays, so let's get it sorted fast. Ask: How would you say this to a board of directors?

Tutor action: Give a short example: We need to address the delays. Let the student respond instantly, even with a partial answer.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Weak verb (make, do, get) + noun → precise verb + noun (make a decision → reach a decision; do research → conduct research; get better → improve)

Examples

  • We need to make a decision by Friday. → We need to reach a decision by Friday.
  • Let's do some research on pricing. → Let's conduct research into pricing.
  • The team got better results. → The team achieved better results.

Execution method: Show each casual sentence beside its formal version. Ask concept-checking questions: Does the new verb show a process or a result? Would the casual verb be natural in a board report? Then ask the student to rewrite one sentence about their own work.

Check question: In a formal report, which verb is more precise: do or conduct?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice at native speed: Our team did a lot of work on the proposal, and we got the client's feedback last week. We made some changes, and now we are getting ready to send it back.

Direct fact check

Ask: What did the team get last week? (the client's feedback). What did they make? (some changes).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is this text formal or informal? Ask the student to point to two informal phrases, such as did a lot of work and getting ready.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Board update: the student gives a short status update on a project to the tutor, who acts as a senior manager. The student must replace at least three weak verbs with precise ones. The tutor asks: What is the next step?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • We have reached ...
  • The team is conducting ...
  • We have achieved ...

Tutor mandate

Act as a conversation partner and language coach. Share one more precise verb live in the chat each time the student uses a weak one, without stopping their flow.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • We did a lot of research and got better results.
  • We made a decision about the budget.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did the student defend a stance without translating from their native language?

☐ Did I share better vocabulary live while the student spoke?

☐ Is the student talking for 75-85% of the class?

☐ Did the wrap-up review good vs. better word choices?
