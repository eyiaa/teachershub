1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Show a simple line graph of monthly revenue with a dip in the middle. Ask: What is the main trend you see, and how confident are you in it?

Tutor action: Let the student describe the trend, then ask how they would qualify it if the data were preliminary.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Trend + precise verb or noun (a marked increase, a slight decline, levelled off) + hedge (roughly, approximately, it appears that) + passive reporting (it has been estimated that ...)

Examples

  • Revenue rose by approximately fifteen percent in the second quarter.
  • There was a marked decline in demand, although it appears to have levelled off.
  • It has been estimated that the trend will continue, provided that the market stays stable.

Execution method: Show a trend with a precise description and a hedged version. Ask guiding questions: Is the figure exact or approximate? Does the hedge show uncertainty about the trend or about the figure? Then ask the student to describe the graph with one precise verb and one hedge.

Check question: In approximately fifteen percent, is the figure exact or an estimate?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this data commentary twice at native speed: Sales rose by roughly eight percent in the first half of the year, although the increase was uneven. It has been suggested that the recovery will continue, but the sample of customers was small, so the findings should be treated with caution.

Direct fact check

Ask: By how much did sales rise? (roughly eight percent). What was uneven? (the increase). Why should the findings be treated with caution? (the sample of customers was small).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is the writer confident or cautious about the recovery? Ask the student to point to the phrases that show caution, such as it has been suggested and should be treated with caution.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Data briefing: the tutor shares a simple table of three quarterly figures with one anomaly. The student describes the trends using precise verbs, qualifies each claim, and explains what further data would be needed before making a recommendation. The tutor, as a finance director, asks: How confident are you in that conclusion?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • Approximately ...
  • There was a marked ...
  • It appears that ..., although ...

Tutor mandate

Act as a finance director who questions every claim. Press for the source of each figure, and share one more precise hedging phrase in the chat when the student overstates a trend.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • Sales are going up a lot, it is definitely the best quarter.
  • The data shows the recovery will happen for sure.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did the student defend a stance without translating from their native language?

☐ Did I share better vocabulary live while the student spoke?

☐ Is the student talking for 75-85% of the class?

☐ Did the wrap-up review good vs. better word choices?
