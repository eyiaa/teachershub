1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Post this question: What is your biggest weakness as a professional?

Tutor action: Let the student answer instantly, then challenge them with: Would you say that is a weakness or a strength in disguise?

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Hedged admission + action taken: I would argue that I ... / I tend to ... / If I were in the role again, I would ... (second conditional, a reflective hypothetical)

Examples

  • I would argue that my main weakness has been delegating too late.
  • I tend to take on too much, so I now review my priorities every Monday.
  • If I were starting that project again, I would involve the finance team from day one.

Execution method: Show the frame with the three parts labelled. Ask guiding questions: Does this sentence admit a weakness? Does it describe a change? Which form shows a reflection on the past? Then ask the student to answer the warm-up question using the frame.

Check question: In If I were starting that project again, is the speaker describing the past or a hypothetical present?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this interview answer twice at native speed: I would say my biggest gap is in budgeting for large projects, though I have addressed it. If I had been better prepared in my last role, I would have avoided the overspend. I now run a cost review before every launch.

Direct fact check

Ask: What is the speaker's biggest gap? (budgeting for large projects). What would they have avoided? (the overspend). What do they do now? (run a cost review before every launch).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Does the speaker sound defensive or reflective? Ask the student to point to the phrase that shows reflection, such as I would say my biggest gap.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Difficult question round: the tutor plays a hiring manager and asks three questions: What is your biggest weakness? Why is there a gap in your CV? What would your last manager say about you? The student answers each in about one minute, admits something honestly, and describes what they have changed.

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • I would argue that ...
  • I tend to ..., so I now ...
  • If I were in that position again, I would ...

Tutor mandate

Act as a firm but fair hiring manager. Press for specifics when an answer is vague, and share one more precise hedge live in the chat when the student sounds defensive.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • My weakness is I am too perfectionist, which is a strength really.
  • If I was in your place, I will do it differently.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did the student defend a stance without translating from their native language?

☐ Did I share better vocabulary live while the student spoke?

☐ Is the student talking for 75-85% of the class?

☐ Did the wrap-up review good vs. better word choices?
