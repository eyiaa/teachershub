1. Warm-Up & Lead-In

Hook / prompt: Post this question: What makes a company lose its market share over time?

Tutor action: Let the student give one or two reasons, then ask which of them is internal and which is external.

2. Theory (Grammar & Mechanics)

Target structure / formula: Market share (the percentage of a market a firm holds) / downturn (a period of falling activity) / to diversify (to spread risk across products or markets) / a hands-on leadership style (a leader who closely directs teams)

Examples

  • The firm lost market share after a prolonged downturn.
  • To diversify, the company entered two new markets.
  • Her hands-on style worked well in a crisis but less well in growth.

Execution method: Show each term with a short definition and a business example. Ask concept-checking questions: Is market share a number or a feeling? Does diversify reduce risk or increase it? Then ask the student to describe one business trend using two of the terms.

Check question: Does diversifying a business spread its risk or concentrate it?

3. Comprehension (Listening & Reading)

Material / context source: Read this text twice at native speed: The company's market share fell during the downturn. The board wants to diversify into services, and the new chief executive has a hands-on style that some staff find demanding.

Direct fact check

Ask: What fell during the downturn? (market share). What does the board want to do? (diversify into services). How would you describe the new chief executive? (hands-on).

Tone and implied meaning check

Ask: Is the writer approving of the chief executive's style? Ask the student to point to the phrase that shows the mixed view, such as some staff find demanding.

4. Conversation (Speaking & Expressing)

Real-world task: Strategy briefing: the student briefs the tutor, as a board member, on a company's options after a downturn, using at least three target terms. The tutor asks: Which option carries the least risk?

Target phrases and vocabulary

  • The firm needs to recover market share by ...
  • Diversifying would ...
  • Given the downturn, we should ...

Tutor mandate

Act as a board member and coach. Challenge the student to justify each option with a business reason, and share one more precise business term live in the chat when they use a vague word such as things.

5. Delayed Feedback & Wrap-Up

Self-correction prompts

  • The company has less customers, so it lost the share.
  • We should do the business in more places to be safe.

Tutor checklist

☐ Did the student defend a stance without translating from their native language?

☐ Did I share better vocabulary live while the student spoke?

☐ Is the student talking for 75-85% of the class?

☐ Did the wrap-up review good vs. better word choices?
